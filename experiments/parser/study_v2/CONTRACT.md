# Fresh full-line study contract

This research lane leaves the application parser and media-writing behavior unchanged. It compares source-only extraction and review assistance using a new corpus. The previous 1,200 cases are development/regression material, not a new test set.

The target is 500 complete Archive packages: 250 train, 50 validation and 200 sealed test. Original notes and filenames are acquired without audio. Training can include new recordings from prior uploaders. Held-out sources exclude detected relatives of the previous corpus and every training component. Components use uploader, normalized Unicode artist/date, exact notes, distinctive technical lines/shingles, conservative email hashes and independently reviewed source-family edges. Generic gear/repertoire or stock notices alone do not prove shared authorship. Source grouping is heuristic and does not establish that all semantic templates are unseen.

Source-only independent review approves the exact proposed membership hash before held-out annotation. Whole components stay together. The final promotion checks unique IDs, split/group membership, normalized source hashes, filenames and note hashes. Training-only sources are pinned to train. Source-audit revisions retain prior proposals and are forbidden after held-out annotation starts. Source-family and nearest-neighbor audits disclose their bounded coverage.

All notes use canonical decoded UTF-8 with an initial BOM removed and CRLF/CR normalized to LF. Interior blank lines remain; one terminal empty split element is omitted. Annotation display adds numbered prefixes, which are not part of source text or offsets. Every normalized line has coverage. Roles are independent multilabel targets: artist, date, venue, city, title, heading, event, continuation, technical_provenance, other and blank. Unknown roles are explicit disputed targets, never invented other/background negatives. Factual uncertainty preserves a known role. Uncertainty, conflict and applicability to another recording are separate flags. Metadata proposals from flagged source evidence are evaluated as ineligible; gold flags are not an inference-time filter.

Multirole spans use exact quotes in a claimed line. Unique exact matching derives Unicode codepoint offsets; overlapping repeated matches are rejected. Every claimed role must have span support. Original responses remain immutable and separate from derived offsets, canonical unknown-join representations and source-reviewed corrections.

Training labels are source-authored weak labels. Validation/test labels require source authoring and independent source review/correction before predictions. Corrected labels are distinguished from agreement and unresolved references. Agent review is not human certification. Metadata, titles, filename joins and required review decisions have separate recording gold; unknown joins use zero disc/number and an empty title, retaining source-known filename IDs separately. Meaningful cover credits and segue symbols stay in titles. Historical metadata can retain its semantic role while remaining ineligible for the current recording. Technical-only uncertainty does not automatically make concert metadata uncertain.

A fresh external ledger has a **$50 incremental ceiling**, separate from the prior run. The initial allocation is $5 pilot, $10 training, $25 independent labeling/review, $5 adjudication and $5 reserve. The audited implementation assigns $11.50 each to Sol source authoring and independent review, with $2 of that review allocation withheld. All source phases share one clock, ledger, maximum four hosted workers, conservative cache-write/output reservations and durable attempt claims. Revised cache phases retain aggregate stage caps. Transport/accounting failures trip the shared circuit; genuine malformed/incomplete responses remain failures. Credentials are read only from the original explicit `.env` at request time and are never copied or exported.

Model and thresholds use train/validation only. Test execution freezes model, source/gold hashes, enabled roles, parser commits, binaries and runner code before predictions. Reference coverage, per-role recording support, precision, background false positives, metadata-tuple improvement, accepted failures and output/write-plan parity are evaluated separately. At least 95% of nonblank reference lines must be independently reviewed; unresolved lines remain in coverage denominators. Missing complete recording/track gold blocks integration. Observed experiment gates do not automatically authorize application integration, merging or release.

Original notes, full responses, quotes, credentials, ledger and learned model weights stay external. Public reproduction uses source manifests, hashes, text-free label references, safe metrics and code; public exports use explicit whitelists. Complete local and remote verification and the measured outcomes are recorded in the study report.
