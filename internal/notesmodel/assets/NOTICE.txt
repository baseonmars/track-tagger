Track Tagger Tiny BERT notes suggestion model pack

Contains adaptations of the Google uncased two-layer BERT checkpoint
google/bert_uncased_L-2_H-128_A-2, released under Apache License 2.0.
Upstream revision: 30b0a37ccaaa32f332884b96992754e246e48c5f
https://huggingface.co/google/bert_uncased_L-2_H-128_A-2/tree/30b0a37ccaaa32f332884b96992754e246e48c5f

Track Tagger contributors adapted the checkpoint for exact source span
inference, added task heads, quantized the word embeddings and assembled
this model pack. Both full and merged LoRA adaptations are included.
The vocabulary is from the pinned upstream checkpoint.
